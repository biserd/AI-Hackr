All prices are in USD. Tax collection is off. Your subscription renews monthly until cancelled. Manage payment methods, invoices, plan changes and cancellation securely through Stripe.

There are no automatic overage charges. When credits run out, new work pauses. Unused monthly credits expire at the end of the period. Downgrades and cancellation take effect at period end. Upgrades are prorated, with additional credits granted only after the adjustment invoice is paid.
